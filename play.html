How the Play Trainer grades you

  1. You play a full game against three computer opponents. Tiles, seats and dora are random.
  2. Every decision you make (discard, chi / pon / kan, skip, tsumo / ron, kan on your turn) is evaluated by akochan, an open-source mahjong AI running on the server. For each legal option it estimates your expected placement points (EV) for the rest of the game, so it also accounts for the score situation.
  3. A play is a mistake when it loses more than the threshold in Settings (default 0.05 pt, the same as mjai-reviewer) against akochan's best option.
  4. On top of that: calling to open your hand with no possible yaku is wrong, and riichi is only wrong when none of your winning tiles are left. Choosing dama is never graded; for a discard, the better of riichi / dama is used.
  5. "What differs" lists verifiable facts (shanten, tile acceptance, waits, furiten, safety, dora). It does not claim to know akochan's reasoning.
  6. If akochan is offline, decisions are not graded rather than guessed.

Opponents are played by this site's own simpler heuristic engine. akochan is free for personal research and education; see deploy/DEPLOY.md.
