Counting fu

2–81, 9 & honors
TripletOpen+2 fu+4 fu
Closed+4 fu+8 fu
Quad (kan)Open+8 fu+16 fu
Closed+16 fu+32 fu

Start with 20 fu. Add 10 for a closed ron. Add 2 for tsumo, 2 for a yakuhai pair (4 for a double wind pair), and 2 for a penchan, kanchan or tanki wait. Then round up to the nearest 10.

Exceptions: chiitoitsu is always 25 fu; pinfu tsumo is 20 fu (pinfu ron is 30); an open hand with no fu is 30 fu.

Basic points = fu × 2(han + 2). Ron: non-dealer ×4, dealer ×6. Tsumo: non-dealer collects ×1 from each non-dealer and ×2 from the dealer; dealer collects ×2 from everyone. Round each payment up to the nearest 100. * 4 han 30 fu and 3 han 60 fu become mangan when kiriage (rounded-up) mangan is enabled.

How to use

  1. A random winning hand is dealt to you at the bottom of the table. The tile set apart on the right is the winning tile; open melds and kans are shown beside it.
  2. The panel on the right tells you whether you won by Tsumo or Ron, whether you are the dealer, and the hand's han and fu.
  3. Type the points the hand is worth. For a non-dealer tsumo you enter both the non-dealer and the dealer payments; for a dealer tsumo, the amount everyone pays.
  4. Press Check (or Enter). The breakdown explains the fu count, the yaku, and the arithmetic. Press Next (or Enter again) for a new hand.
  5. The Score Chart button opens the full lookup table. Hover Common Yaku / Rare Yaku for reference. Settings let you hide the fu or han so you count them yourself.

The round wind is shown in the top-left box and in the centre of the table; your seat wind is the one facing you on the compass. The dealer is marked with the red 親 chip. Dora indicators are shown in the top-left box.
